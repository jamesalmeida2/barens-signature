Barens email signature

Copy the banner below and paste it into your email signature, under your text signature. The banner links to barens.com.

Where to paste it

  • Gmail: Settings (gear icon) → See all settings → General → Signature. Paste below your text, then scroll down and click Save Changes.
  • Outlook (new or web): Settings → Accounts → Signatures. Paste below your text and click Save.
  • Outlook desktop (Windows): File → Options → Mail → Signatures. Paste below your text and click OK.
  • Apple Mail: Mail → Settings → Signatures. Uncheck “Always match my default message font,” then paste below your text.

After pasting, send a test email to yourself and click the banner to make sure it opens barens.com. If the button doesn’t work in your browser, click and drag across the banner to select it, then copy with Ctrl+C (Windows) or Cmd+C (Mac).
